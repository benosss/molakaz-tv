# Roadmap

What's coming to MoLakaz TV, roughly in order. Ideas and votes are welcome in
[Issues](https://github.com/benosss/molakaz-tv/issues) — ⭐ the repo to follow along.

## Next

- [ ] **Website** — a home for the project: the film, the features, the install guide and the download, in one page.
- [ ] **Game controllers in one step** — pair a PlayStation (DualShock 4 / DualSense) or Xbox controller from
      MoLakaz TV's own Settings: it explains the button to hold on that controller, finds it, pairs it and shows its
      battery. The home screen works with a controller too (A to open, B to go back).
- [ ] **Wider and bigger screens** — layouts made for ultra-wide (21:9, 32:9) and very large screens and projectors:
      more cards in a row, room for the weather and what's playing side by side, and films cropped for the shape of
      the screen.
- [ ] **LG and Samsung out of preview** — tested on more TVs, with the same previews and Continue watching where the
      TVs allow it.

## Later

- [ ] The hour-by-hour and 5-day forecast, one press away
- [ ] Your own photos in the screensaver
- [ ] Move an app on the bar by holding OK on it
- [ ] Continue watching from Android's Watch Next list (every app that publishes it)
- [ ] More boxes and TVs tested (Chromecast with Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Xiaomi, TCL)

## Done

- [x] **1.1** — songs come alive (live, cover-coloured scenes), glass icons (glass, tinted or original), 28 languages,
      updates from GitHub, a safety net that brings the original home screen back after repeated crashes, TV inputs and
      HDMI on the bar, an LG and Samsung preview, Fresh picks twice a day, and smoother film changes.
- [x] **1.0** — living nature films, previews on focus (YouTube, trailers, Spotify songs), Continue watching, Fresh
      picks, Now playing with a live visualiser, phone remote, Goodnight timer, ambient sound, a first-run set-up from
      a USB stick (no computer), and a one-press way back to the original home screen.
